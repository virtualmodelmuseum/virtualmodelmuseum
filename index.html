Virtual Model Museum

This collection was begun by my father nearly seventy years ago, and has been quietly gathering character ever since.

The aim is not to parade perfection, but to offer a fond look at the many agreeable corners of scale modelling: aircraft, motor cars, military vehicles, and those small mechanical characters that make the hobby so endlessly charming.
